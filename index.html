About & Help Guide

Everything you need to know about how QR code generation and scanning work in QRpop.

How QR Code Generation Works

Step-by-step creation & format guide
  • 1. Select Content Type: Choose from Plain Text, URLs, Wi-Fi networks, Contact vCards, Phone numbers, or Emails.
  • 2. Enter Information: Fill in the required fields. The live QR preview updates in real-time.
  • 3. Customize Appearance: Adjust foreground & background colors, pick a quick theme, or choose smooth rounded/dotted corner shapes.
  • 4. Download or Share: Export high-resolution PNG or vector SVG files, or copy directly to your clipboard.

How QR Code Scanning Works

Camera feed & image upload decoding
  • Live Camera Feed: Switch to the Scan tab to automatically request camera permission. Hold the QR code within view for instant detection.
  • Image File Upload: Drag & drop or select PNG, JPG, or SVG image files stored on your device.
  • Smart Payload Actions: Automatically recognizes links (Open Link), phone numbers (Call), emails (Compose), Wi-Fi passwords, and contact cards.
  • Scan Again: Pause after detection and tap "Scan Again" anytime to scan another code.

Privacy & Performance Guarantee

QRpop is built entirely client-side. Your camera stream, uploaded QR images, Wi-Fi passwords, and contact information are processed directly in your browser without ever being sent to a remote server.
